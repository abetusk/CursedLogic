Spiral calibration (image pixels)
A′ is the point that A lands on after one step (the same figure or shape, smaller, further in along the arm). The step's angle is only known up to whole turns; if A′ is one full turn further along the arm, set extra turns to 1 (or -1 for the other direction). In the log-polar view a true logarithmic spiral is a straight line, and the dashed green line should follow the painted arm.
